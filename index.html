How it works and limits
  • Grid ↔ latitude/longitude uses the Krüger series to 6th order (as in PROJ), accurate to well under a millimetre anywhere in Nova Scotia. The NS converter workbook uses shorter series and agrees within about 1–2.5 mm.
  • ATS77 ↔ NAD83(CSRS) v6, epoch 2010 uses the Province's NTv2 grid NS778302.GSB (EPSG:9106) with bilinear interpolation – built in, or loaded once from your device. The grid's own accuracy is decimetre level (metre level in places) – fine for mapping and relocating evidence, not a replacement for a new NAD83(CSRS) tie. The grid is licensed: share this app only as the licence allows.
  • No height or epoch change is applied. Latitude/longitude are on the datum shown.
  • Input: decimal degrees or DMS (44 53 13.81, 44°53′13.81″N, 44:53:13.81). Longitude is West unless it contains E; positive numbers are read as West. MTM eastings include the 25… / 24… (NS 2010) or 5… / 4… (ATS77) prefix.
  • Scale factor is the point scale factor of the output grid; convergence γ is positive east of the central meridian (true bearing = grid bearing + γ).
  • Saved points stay on this device in this browser only.
